Privacy Policy

Handpan Sheet Music (iPad) · Last updated: 25 September 2026

Handpan Sheet Music does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The sheets you write are saved as .hpsheet files on your iPad, in the app's folder of the Files app. Your settings (theme, language, palette) are saved on your iPad too. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else: the app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network requests.

Sharing

When you share a sheet (as a file or a PDF) the iOS share sheet sends it only where you choose, for example AirDrop, Mail or Messages.

Backups

Your sheets can be part of your iPad's iCloud or computer backups, and you can copy them to iCloud Drive yourself. Those are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/lemunozm/handpan-sheet-music/issues

Política de privacidad (español)

Handpan Sheet Music no recoge, guarda ni comparte ningún dato personal. Tus hojas y ajustes se guardan solo en tu iPad; la app no tiene cuentas, analíticas ni anuncios y no se conecta a internet. Al compartir una hoja, iOS la envía únicamente a donde tú elijas. Contacto: abre una incidencia en github.com/lemunozm/handpan-sheet-music/issues
